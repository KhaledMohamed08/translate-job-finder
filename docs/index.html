How matches work

Each job gets a score out of 100. It goes up for a translation role, Arabic, French or Korean, remote work or a job in Egypt, and international organizations. It goes down for senior roles, long experience requirements and other languages. Outside Egypt, general jobs only appear if they are close to language work. Always confirm eligibility on the original posting.
